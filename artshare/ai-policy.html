ArtShare Human-Creation Policy

ArtShare is a human-only art sharing platform. AI-generated or AI-assisted final artwork is prohibited.

By uploading content, you certify original human authorship, maintain process evidence on request, and accept moderation enforcement including flagging and removal.

Repeated violations may lead to account suspension when backend enforcement is connected.
